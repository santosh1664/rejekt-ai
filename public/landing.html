FAQ

Questions, answered.

What kinds of jobs can I find?

Roles in engineering, product, design, data, operations, sales and more, at 50,000+ companies, from early-stage startups to the largest employers.

Will it apply to jobs without asking me?

Only if you turn auto-apply on. You can require your review for any kind of role, and set daily and per-company limits.

How often do new jobs show up?

Every 6 hours. New openings land in your matches as soon as we find them, and roles that close drop out on their own.

Which countries are covered?

Jobs in the US, Canada, the EU and Australia, including remote roles open to those locations.

What happens when a form asks something it doesn't know?

It stops and puts the application in Needs you, with the exact question. Answer once and it remembers for next time.

Why is it invite-only?

We're letting people in gradually so every application is done right. Request an invite below and we'll get in touch when there's a spot.
