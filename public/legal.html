AI disclosure

Effective

In short: Rejekt.ai uses AI to tailor your resume and to draft answers to application questions. It ranks jobs with rules we wrote, not AI. You can see every change it makes, require your approval before anything is sent, and you stay responsible for what goes out in your name.

Where we use AI

FeatureWhat the AI doesWhat you control
Resume tailoringRewrites and reorders your resume's bullet points and summary to match a job's wording.Every change is highlighted (“What changed”). You can edit the resume or apply without tailoring.
Application answersDrafts answers to form questions that your profile and saved answers don't cover (for example “Why do you want to work here?”).Require review before submitting, and edit any answer. Questions about facts only you know go to “Needs you” instead.
Choosing optionsPicks the closest option in a drop-down when your saved answer is worded differently.Review before submitting.

Not AI: job matching and match scores, the jobs we show you, and your work authorization, sponsorship and demographic answers come from rules and from your own profile, not from an AI model's judgment.

What we send to the AI provider

To tailor a resume or draft an answer, we send the AI provider the relevant parts of your resume and profile, the job description, and the question being answered. We use OpenRouter, which routes requests to the model provider we choose (currently DeepSeek V3). We don't send your password, job-site passwords, Gmail data or demographic answers to the AI provider. We choose providers and settings that don't use your data to train their models, and we don't use your data to train models either.

What AI can get wrong

  • It can overstate, misstate or leave out details. We instruct it not to invent experience, but it can still make mistakes.
  • It can misread a question or pick the wrong option.
  • That's why changes are shown to you, why you can require approval, and why you remain responsible for what's sent in your name.

Automated decisions

Rejekt.ai doesn't make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects. It decides which jobs to show you and, if you turn auto-apply on, which matching jobs to apply to within your rules. You can change those rules or turn auto-apply off at any time. Employers make their own hiring decisions, and some use their own AI tools to screen applications; their practices are covered by their own notices.
